Exact Algebraic Bridge to wildberger and euclid¶

  • Greek *Dynamis* = Wildberger’s Quadrance (`geometor/wildberger`): Hambidge’s recovery of Greek dynamis (“square / power” in Plato’s Theaetetus 147d–148b and Euclid Book X Def. 2, euclid:elements) translates with 100% algebraic precision into Rational Trigonometry (geometor/wildberger):

    • A rectangle of unit height ($Q_{\text{end}} = 1$) and horizontal side $s_n = \sqrt{n}$ has horizontal quadrance $Q_n = n$.

    • By Pythagoras’s Theorem ($Q_{\text{diag}} = Q_{\text{end}} + Q_n = 1 + n$), swinging the diagonal down to the baseline increments the side quadrance by exact integer steps $Q = 1 \to 2 \to 3 \to 4 \to 5$!

    • Moreover, for any rectangle of aspect ratio $r$ with main diagonal vector $(r, 1)$ and reciprocal rectangle of width $1/r$ with counter-diagonal vector $(1/r, -1)$, their dot product is:

      $$(r, 1) \cdot \left(\frac{1}{r}, -1\right) = 1 - 1 = 0$$

      proving algebraically for every ratio $r > 0$ that the diagonal of the reciprocal intersects the diagonal of the whole at exact perpendicular spread $s = 1$ ($90^\circ$)!
