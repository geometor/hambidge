## Mission

`geometor/hambidge` is part of the interconnected [GEOMETOR](https://geometor.com) semantic digital archive. It liberates Jay Hambidge's pioneering formulation of *Dynamic Symmetry* from isolated print bounds into addressable semantic units, calibrated theme-adaptive vector diagrams, primary-source folio provenance, and exact symbolic models of root rectangles ($\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3}, \sqrt{4}, \sqrt{5}$), whirling squares, and golden-ratio proportions across the GEOMETOR corpus.

## Books in This Archive

- [The Elements of Dynamic Symmetry](https://geometor.github.io/hambidge/dynamic_symmetry/) — The foundational 1920 treatise establishing the geometry of dynamic rectangles, reciprocal proportions, and thematic partitioning in Greek art and nature.
