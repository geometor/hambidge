The Elements of Dynamic Symmetry
================================

.. rubric:: Jay Hambidge — Yale University Press (*The Diagonal*, 1919–1920 & *Dynamic Symmetry: The Greek Vase*, 1920)

.. container:: title-page

   | **Author:** :person:`Jay Hambidge <hambidge>` (1867–1924)
   | **Primary Editions:** *The Diagonal* (Vol. I, Nos. 1–12, 1919–1920) & *Dynamic Symmetry: The Greek Vase* (New Haven: Yale University Press, 1920).
   | **Digital Critical Edition:** Synchronized 3-track edition (*English · Summary · Notes*) with calibrated vector diagrams and exact symbolic geometry.

.. epigraph::

   “The Greeks said that such lines were *not* irrational in power, because they were commensurable in square. This is really the great secret of Greek design. In understanding this measurableness of area instead of line the Greek artists had command of an infinity of beautiful shapes which modern artists are unable to use.”

   — :person:`Jay Hambidge <hambidge>`, :cite:`*The Diagonal*, Vol. I, No. 1, p. 15 <hambidge:1920-diagonal>`
