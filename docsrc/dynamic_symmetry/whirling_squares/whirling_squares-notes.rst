Exact $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5})$ Decomposition (`geometor/divine`)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Over the golden quadratic field $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5})$ (`geometor/divine`), where $\phi = \frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}$ and $\phi^{-1} = \phi - 1 = \frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{2}$, Hambidge's dissections embody three exact identities:

$$\sqrt{5} = 2\phi - 1 = 1 + 2\phi^{-1} = \phi + \phi^{-1}$$

1. $\sqrt{5} = 1 + 2\phi^{-1}$: A central unit square flanked symmetrically by two vertical reciprocal golden rectangles;
2. $\sqrt{5} = \phi + \phi^{-1}$: A horizontal whirling-square rectangle ($\phi$) joined to a vertical reciprocal golden rectangle ($\phi^{-1}$);
3. $2\phi - \sqrt{5} = 1$: Two overlapping whirling-square rectangles sharing a central unit square!

Pole of the Whirling Squares (The Spiral Eye)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The intersection $(x_0, y_0)$ of the main diagonal $y = x / \phi$ and the reciprocal diagonal $y = 1 - \phi x$ gives the exact pole coordinates:

$$x_0 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}, \quad y_0 = \frac{5 - \sqrt{5}}{10}$$

proving that the pole of the logarithmic spiral whirling through the nested square vertices lies at an exact algebraic point in $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5})$.
