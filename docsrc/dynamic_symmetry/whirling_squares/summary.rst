The Root-Five Rectangle & The Whirling Squares — Summary
========================================================

.. rubric:: Analytical Synopsis — *The Elements of Dynamic Symmetry*, Lesson I (*The Diagonal*, 1919, pp. 15–17)

Overview of Section III
-----------------------

Lesson I develops the primary dynamic harmonic shape derived from plant phyllotaxis—the :math:`\sqrt{5}` rectangle—and its constituent Golden Rectangle:

1. **The Root-Five Rectangle** (:math:`1 : \sqrt{5}`): The square on the end is exactly one-fifth of the square on the side. Constructed by bisecting the unit square and swinging the half-diagonal :math:`\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}` to both sides of the base.
2. **Decomposition into Golden Rectangles**: The :math:`\sqrt{5}` rectangle consists of a central unit square flanked symmetrically by two reciprocal golden rectangles (:math:`\phi^{-1} \approx 0.618034`): :math:`\sqrt{5} = 1 + 2\phi^{-1}`.
3. **The Rectangle of the Whirling Squares** (:math:`1 : \phi`): Constructed by swinging the half-diagonal to one side (:math:`1 : \frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}`).
4. **Reciprocals & Gnomonic Whirling Squares**: The reciprocal rectangle of ratio :math:`1/\phi = \phi - 1` cuts the parent diagonal at :math:`90^\circ`. Successive detachment of square gnomons forms a spiral whirling toward the pole of perpendicular diagonals.
5. **Two Golden Rectangles Overlapping**: Two overlapping :math:`1 : \phi` rectangles sharing a central unit square reconstitute the parent :math:`\sqrt{5}` rectangle: :math:`2\phi - 1 = \sqrt{5}`.
