Exact :math:`\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5})` Decomposition (`geometor/divine`)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Over the golden quadratic field :math:`\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5})` (`geometor/divine`), where :math:`\phi = \frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}` and :math:`\phi^{-1} = \phi - 1 = \frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{2}`, Hambidge's dissections embody three exact identities:

.. math::

   \sqrt{5} = 2\phi - 1 = 1 + 2\phi^{-1} = \phi + \phi^{-1}

1. :math:`\sqrt{5} = 1 + 2\phi^{-1}`: A central unit square flanked symmetrically by two vertical reciprocal golden rectangles;
2. :math:`\sqrt{5} = \phi + \phi^{-1}`: A horizontal whirling-square rectangle (:math:`\phi`) joined to a vertical reciprocal golden rectangle (:math:`\phi^{-1}`);
3. :math:`2\phi - \sqrt{5} = 1`: Two overlapping whirling-square rectangles sharing a central unit square!

Pole of the Whirling Squares (The Spiral Eye)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The intersection :math:`(x_0, y_0)` of the main diagonal :math:`y = x / \phi` and the reciprocal diagonal :math:`y = 1 - \phi x` gives the exact pole coordinates:

.. math::

   x_0 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}, \quad y_0 = \frac{5 - \sqrt{5}}{10}

proving that the pole of the logarithmic spiral whirling through the nested square vertices lies at an exact algebraic point in :math:`\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5})`.
