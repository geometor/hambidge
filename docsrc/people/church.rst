Church
======

**Arthur Harry Church** (1865 – 1937) — *British Botanist & Morphological Illustrator (Oxford)*
   Author of *On the Relation of Phyllotaxis to Mechanical Laws* (:cite:`church:1904`), whose precision diagrams of intersecting orthogonal logarithmic spirals in sunflower capitula ($34/55$, $55/89$, $89/144$) directly inspired both :person:`D'Arcy Thompson's <thompson>` morphology and Lesson I of Jay Hambidge's Dynamic Symmetry.
