Kepler
======

**Johannes Kepler** (1571 – 1630) — *German Astronomer, Mathematician & Natural Philosopher*
   Celebrated the golden ratio alongside Pythagoras's Theorem as the two great treasures of geometry in *Strena seu de Nive Sexangula* (:cite:`kepler:1611`), quoted by Hambidge in Lesson II of *The Elements of Dynamic Symmetry*.
