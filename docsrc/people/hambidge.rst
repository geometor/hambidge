Hambidge
========

**Jay Hambidge** (1867 – 1924) — *Theorist of Design & Geometer of Dynamic Symmetry*
   Canadian-American artist and geometric scholar who rediscovered the Greek canon of proportional areas based on Euclidean commensurability in square (:cite:`euclid:elements`, Book X Def. 2), publishing *The Diagonal* (1919–1920) and *Dynamic Symmetry: The Greek Vase* (1920) at Yale University Press.
