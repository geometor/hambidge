Euclid
======

**Euclid of Alexandria** (fl. c. 300 BC) — *Alexandrian Greek Mathematician (Author of the Elements)*
   Provides the axiomatic bedrock of Dynamic Symmetry: Book I Prop. 47 (Pythagoras's Theorem), Book II Def. 2 (gnomon), Book II Prop. 11 & Book VI Prop. 30 (extreme and mean ratio :math:`\phi`), and especially Book X Def. 2 (magnitudes commensurable in square, *dynamei symmetroi*)—the literal Greek source of Hambidge's 'dynamic' proportion.
