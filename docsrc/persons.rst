.. _persons:

======
People
======

.. rubric:: Prosopographical Registry of Design Theorists, Geometers, Botanists & Commentators

.. _person-hambidge:

**Jay Hambidge** (1867 – 1924) — *Theorist of Design & Geometer of Dynamic Symmetry*
   Canadian-American artist and geometric scholar who rediscovered the Greek canon of proportional areas based on Euclidean commensurability in square (:cite:`euclid:elements`, Book X Def. 2), publishing *The Diagonal* (1919–1920) and *Dynamic Symmetry: The Greek Vase* (1920) at Yale University Press.

.. _person-church:

**Arthur Harry Church** (1865 – 1937) — *British Botanist & Morphological Illustrator (Oxford)*
   Author of *On the Relation of Phyllotaxis to Mechanical Laws* (:cite:`church:1904`), whose precision diagrams of intersecting orthogonal logarithmic spirals in sunflower capitula ($34/55$, $55/89$, $89/144$) directly inspired both :person:`D'Arcy Thompson's <thompson>` morphology and Lesson I of Jay Hambidge's Dynamic Symmetry.

.. _person-euclid:

**Euclid of Alexandria** (fl. c. 300 BC) — *Alexandrian Greek Mathematician (Author of the Elements)*
   Provides the axiomatic bedrock of Dynamic Symmetry: Book I Prop. 47 (Pythagoras's Theorem), Book II Def. 2 (gnomon), Book II Prop. 11 & Book VI Prop. 30 (extreme and mean ratio $\phi$), and especially Book X Def. 2 (magnitudes commensurable in square, *dynamei symmetroi*)—the literal Greek source of Hambidge's 'dynamic' proportion.

.. _person-kepler:

**Johannes Kepler** (1571 – 1630) — *German Astronomer, Mathematician & Natural Philosopher*
   Celebrated the golden ratio alongside Pythagoras's Theorem as the two great treasures of geometry in *Strena seu de Nive Sexangula* (:cite:`kepler:1611`), quoted by Hambidge in Lesson II of *The Elements of Dynamic Symmetry*.

.. _person-thompson:

**Sir D'Arcy Wentworth Thompson** (1860 – 1948) — *Scottish Mathematical Biologist & Classical Scholar (St Andrews)*
   Author of *On Growth and Form* (:cite:`thompson:1917`), whose analysis of equiangular spirals, gnomonic growth in molluscan shells, and botanical phyllotaxis directly complements Hambidge's synthetic dynamic symmetry and root rectangles.

.. _person-plato:

**Plato** (c. 428 – 348 BC) — *Athenian Philosopher & Founder of the Academy*
   In the *Theaetetus* (147d–148b), records Theodorus of Cyrene's demonstration of the linear incommensurability of roots from $\sqrt{3}$ to $\sqrt{17}$ and introduces the concept of *dynamis* (square power) from which commensurability in square derives.

.. _person-phiarchitect:

**phi ARCHITECT** (contemporary) — *Founder of GEOMETOR & Digital Scriptorium Curator*
   Creator of the GEOMETOR Digital Scriptorium editions, exact symbolic geometry models, and semantic cross-repository apparatus.
