~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Rope-Stretchers & Pythagoras's Theorem (`geometor/euclid`)**: Hambidge's account of the Egyptian *harpedonaptae* using a 12-knot rope :math:`(3^2 + 4^2 = 5^2)` connects empirical nilotic surveying directly to :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` *Elements* I.47 and Book II.
