Thompson
========

**Sir D'Arcy Wentworth Thompson** (1860 – 1948) — *Scottish Mathematical Biologist & Classical Scholar (St Andrews)*
   Author of *On Growth and Form* (:cite:`thompson:1917`), whose analysis of equiangular spirals, gnomonic growth in molluscan shells, and botanical phyllotaxis directly complements Hambidge's synthetic dynamic symmetry and root rectangles.
